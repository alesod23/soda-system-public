# Approval cards stay short

The owner reads cards on a phone. The first line names the decision ("Send the follow-up to the supplier?"),
then the artifact itself (the draft, the diff), then one line of why. Never a wall of context above the question.

Why: a card that needs scrolling before the question gets answered late or not at all.
