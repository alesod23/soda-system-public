## The rules in front

### Structure

**R1.** The ask goes first, subject included; propose one concrete time instead of asking for availability. *e.g.* Subject "Move Thursday's call to 15:00?" *liked (H8):* "Could we move Thursday's call to 15:00? Something came up at 14:00 on my side." [ledger H1,H7 +like H8]

**R2.** At most five sentences for a first message. [ledger H2]

### Voice

**R3.** No filler openers. *e.g.* start with the point, not "I hope this finds you well" [ledger H4]

**R4.** Write in the language the person last wrote in. [ledger H5]

**R5.** Never claim an attachment or a link that is not actually there. [ledger H6]

3. Verify every attachment and link you mention exists (R5).
